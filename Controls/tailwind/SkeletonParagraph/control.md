Renders an animated placeholder shaped like a paragraph of text. The last line is shorter to mimic a real paragraph.

See also [SkeletonCard](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonCard), [SkeletonList](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonList), and [SkeletonTable](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonTable).

> The `SkeletonParagraph` control is new in DotVVM 5.1.
