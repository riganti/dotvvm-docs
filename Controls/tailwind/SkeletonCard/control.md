Renders an animated placeholder shaped like a content card with an image, a title, text lines, and an author footer. Display it while the real content is loading.

See also [SkeletonList](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonList), [SkeletonParagraph](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonParagraph), and [SkeletonTable](~/controls/tailwind/SkeletonTable).

> The `SkeletonCard` control is new in DotVVM 5.1.
