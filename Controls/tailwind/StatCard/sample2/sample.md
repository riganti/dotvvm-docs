## Sample 2: Progress, ring and badge

The `Progress` property (0 to 100) renders a progress bar below the value. The bar has a label (`ProgressLabel`, localized "Progress" by default) and an optional `ProgressDescription` below it.

In the `Ring` layout, the progress is displayed as a circular ring next to the value.

The `BadgeText` property renders a pill in the bottom right corner. `AccentColor` sets the color of the progress bar, ring, and badge.
