## Sample 4: Help texts, placeholders and MultiSelectFormField

Use `HelpText` (or `HelpTemplate`) to display a hint below the input, and `Placeholder` to show a text in an empty input. The `MultiSelectFormField` wraps the [MultiSelect](~/controls/tailwind/MultiSelect) control and supports all its properties.

HTML attributes with the `Control-` prefix are rendered on the input element.

> The `MultiSelectFormField` control, the `HelpText`, `HelpTemplate`, and `Placeholder` properties, and the `Control-` attribute prefix are new in DotVVM 5.1.
